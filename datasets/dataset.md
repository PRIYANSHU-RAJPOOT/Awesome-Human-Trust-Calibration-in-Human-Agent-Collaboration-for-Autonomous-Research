# Datasets

This page contains datasets and benchmark resources relevant to human–AI interaction, trust in automation, explainable AI, and autonomous research systems.

> Note: Direct, standardized datasets specifically focused on **trust calibration in autonomous research agents** are still limited. Therefore, this collection includes relevant datasets and benchmark resources from closely related areas such as human–AI interaction, explainability, machine learning, and language-model evaluation.

## 1. UCI Machine Learning Repository

**Source:** University of California, Irvine

**Description:** A large collection of datasets used for machine learning research and experimentation.

**Application to this topic:** These datasets can be used to build AI systems whose reliability, uncertainty, explanations, and performance can be studied in human–AI trust experiments.

[Access Resource](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/)

## 2. OpenML

**Source:** OpenML

**Description:** An open platform containing datasets, machine learning tasks, experiments, and benchmark results.

**Application to this topic:** OpenML can support reproducible experiments involving AI performance, model comparison, and human reliance on algorithmic systems.

[Access Resource](https://www.openml.org/)

## 3. Hugging Face Datasets

**Source:** Hugging Face

**Description:** A collection of datasets for natural language processing, machine learning, and AI research.

**Application to this topic:** Language and question-answering datasets can be used to evaluate autonomous or agentic AI systems performing research-related tasks such as information retrieval, summarization, and reasoning.

[Access Resource](https://huggingface.co/datasets)

## Future Dataset Directions

Future research should develop datasets specifically designed to study:

* Human trust calibration in autonomous agents
* Appropriate reliance on AI recommendations
* Human responses to AI errors
* Trust changes during repeated human–agent interaction
* The effect of explanations on reliance
* The effect of uncertainty communication on trust
* Source provenance and evidence verification in AI-assisted research
