# Review-to-experiment gap map

| Review gap | Executable interpretation | Feasibility | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-step error propagation | Inject controlled retrieval corruption and trace downstream evidence/label errors | High with sparse retrieval | Primary |
| Adaptive human checkpoints | Add a label-blind evidence validation/recovery step | Moderate | Paired intervention |
| Provenance/evidence interfaces | Surface source IDs and support indicators | Moderate | Future extension |
| Explanations and calibrated acceptance | Compare explanations and correct/incorrect acceptance | Human study required | Not selected |
| Dynamic trust measurement | Repeated trust/reliance measures over interactions | Participants and validated measures required | Future |
| Expertise sensitivity | Compare novice and expert behavior | Participant recruitment required | Future |
| Long-term interaction | Longitudinal human-agent study | High burden | Future |
| Evaluation beyond accuracy | Add evidence quality, propagation, recovery and efficiency | Moderate | Secondary outcomes |

These gaps are stated in the supplied review. This mapping is a proposed operationalization; it does not prove that the exact experiment is novel.
